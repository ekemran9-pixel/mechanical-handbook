Chemicals & SDS

Reading safety data sheets and labels, common maintenance chemicals and their hazards, storage and spills.

On this page

Safety data sheet (SDS) — 16 sections

Most useful for the crew: Section 2 (hazards), 4 (first aid), 5 (fire), 6 (spills), 7 (handling and storage), 8 (exposure limits and PPE), 10 (stability, incompatibles).

GHS pictograms

  • Flame: flammable. Flame over circle: oxidiser. Exploding bomb: explosive.
  • Corrosion: burns skin and eyes, corrodes metal.
  • Skull and crossbones: acute toxicity.
  • Health hazard (person with star): cancer, organ damage, sensitiser.
  • Exclamation mark: irritant, harmful.
  • Gas cylinder: gas under pressure.
  • Environment (dead tree and fish).

Common maintenance chemicals

ProductHazard
Degreasers and solvents (acetone, IPA, brake cleaner)Flammable, vapours affect the brain, skin defatting
Thread lockers, adhesivesSkin sensitisers
Epoxy resins and hardenersSensitisers (dermatitis, asthma)
Isocyanate paints (PU), foamsAsthma; air-fed mask for spraying
Pickling paste (HF/nitric)Severe burns, HF absorbs through skin (calcium gluconate)
Caustic soda, acidsBurns, eye damage
Used oilSkin cancer with long contact, environmental
Penetrant and developer spraysFlammable aerosols

Rules

  • Read the SDS before using a new product. Keep SDSs where the crew can find them.
  • Labels on every container, including small decanted bottles.
  • Store flammables in a flammable cabinet; separate acids from caustics and oxidisers from fuels.
  • Spill kits at the work area. Report spills to the environment.
  • Eyewash within 10 seconds' walk of corrosive work.
