Lockout / tagout & isolation

Energy isolation steps, isolation methods (single valve, double block and bleed, positive isolation), group lockout and try-out.

On this page
  1. Seven steps
  2. Isolation methods for process fluids (best to least)
  3. Group lockout
  4. Rules

Seven steps

  1. Prepare: identify all energy sources from drawings and the plant: electrical, mechanical, hydraulic, pneumatic, process pressure, thermal, chemical, gravity, stored (springs, accumulators, capacitors).
  2. Notify: tell operations and everyone affected.
  3. Shut down the equipment by normal stop.
  4. Isolate: open breakers (rack out), close and lock valves, fit blinds, disconnect couplings, block moving parts.
  5. Lock and tag: each worker applies a personal lock (or the group lock box method).
  6. Release stored energy: depressurise, drain, vent, bleed, block or lower loads, discharge capacitors, let it cool.
  7. Verify: try to start the equipment (then return the switch to OFF), check gauges read zero, test for dead (electrician), open drain to confirm.

Isolation methods for process fluids (best to least)

MethodUse
Positive isolation: remove a spool or insert a spade/spectacle blindConfined space entry, hot work on lines, long-term isolation, hazardous fluids
Double block and bleed (DBB): two closed valves with an open bleed betweenMost line breaks on hazardous fluids
Single valve with bleedLower hazard
Single valveLow hazard, low pressure, non-toxic only (by site rule)

Group lockout

  • Operations locks all isolation points with the group locks. Keys go into a lock box.
  • The lock box is locked by every worker with their personal lock.
  • Nobody can remove the isolations until every worker removes their lock.

Rules

  • One person, one lock, one key. Never lend your key.
  • Never remove someone else's lock. Lost key or absent worker: follow the site's lock removal procedure (manager approval, search for the person).
  • Tags alone are not isolation. They are a warning.
  • Check isolation again after a shift change or long break.
  • Pumps: also isolate suction and discharge (backflow can spin the pump), seal flush, cooling water and steam tracing.
