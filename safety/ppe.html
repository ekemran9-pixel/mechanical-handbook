PPE

Choosing and using PPE for mechanical work: head, eyes, face, hearing, hands, respiratory, body and feet.

Body partPPENotes
HeadHard hat (EN 397 / ANSI Z89.1)Replace after impact or 3–5 years. Chin strap for work at height
EyesSafety glasses with side shields (Z87+, EN 166)Always on site. Goggles for chemicals and dust
FaceFace shield over glassesGrinding, chemicals, line breaking, hydrojetting
HearingPlugs or muffsRequired above 85 dB(A). Double up above ~105 dB
HandsGloves by hazardCut level (EN 388 A–F, ANSI A1–A9), chemical (by glove chart), welding leather, impact backed. No gloves at lathes and drills
LungsRespiratorsFFP2/FFP3 or N95/P100 for dust and fume; half mask with cartridges for vapours; supplied air for unknown or IDLH. Fit test and clean shave
BodyFR coveralls, chemical suitsFR in hydrocarbon plants. Chemical suits by hazard
FeetSafety boots (EN ISO 20345 S3, ASTM F2413)Toe cap, midsole, slip resistance. Metatarsal guards for heavy work
FallHarness and lanyardsSee working at height

PPE is the last line of defence. If you rely on PPE alone, look again for better controls.
