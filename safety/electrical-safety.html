Electrical safety for mechanics

What mechanical crews need to know about electrical hazards: isolation, portable tools, welding earths and hazardous areas.

  • Only authorised electricians isolate and prove dead. Mechanical crews apply their own lock after.
  • Portable tools: 110 V centre-tapped earth (UK) or RCD/GFCI protected supplies. Inspect cords and plugs. Tested and tagged.
  • Welding return lead clamped close to the weld. Stray welding current through bearings, instrument cables and earthing systems causes damage and shocks.
  • Keep 3 m or more from overhead lines (check local rules by voltage) with cranes, scaffold tubes and ladders.

Hazardous areas (ATEX / IECEx)

ZoneGas present
Zone 0Continuously or for long periods (inside tanks)
Zone 1Likely in normal operation
Zone 2Unlikely, only briefly
  • Only certified Ex equipment in zones (marked Ex d, Ex e, Ex i, etc.).
  • Phones, cameras, non-rated torches and battery tools need a hot work or special permit with gas monitoring.
  • Don't open Ex enclosures or alter cable glands: that's for Ex-trained electricians.

Arc flash

Electrical faults in switchgear can release huge heat and pressure. Only trained people enter switch rooms during racking or live work, in arc-rated PPE. Mechanical crews stay outside the arc flash boundary.
