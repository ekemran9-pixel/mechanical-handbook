Emergency response

Alarms, muster, headcount, fire extinguisher selection, first aid basics and spill response.

On this page
  1. On alarm
  2. Fire extinguishers
  3. First aid basics (trained first aiders act)
  4. Spills

On alarm

  1. Stop work, make safe in seconds (close the torch, stop the machine).
  2. Go to the muster point by the safe route (check wind direction for gas alarms).
  3. Foreman: headcount against the crew list. Report missing people at once to the muster controller.
  4. Stay until released.

Fire extinguishers

TypeGood forNot for
Water (red)Class A: wood, paper, clothElectrical, flammable liquids
Foam (cream label)A and B (liquids)Live electrical (most types)
Dry powder (blue label)A, B, C (gases), electricalIndoors small spaces (visibility)
CO₂ (black label)Electrical, BOutdoors in wind, confined spaces (asphyxiation)
Wet chemical (yellow)Class F cooking oils

PASS: Pull pin, Aim at base of fire, Squeeze, Sweep. Keep your back to an exit. Only fight small fires you can put out quickly.

First aid basics (trained first aiders act)

  • Burns: cool with running water for 20 minutes. Don't remove stuck clothing. Cover loosely with cling film.
  • Eye injuries: flush with eyewash for at least 15 minutes (chemicals). Don't rub. Don't remove embedded objects.
  • Bleeding: direct pressure.
  • Unconscious, not breathing: call for help, CPR, AED.
  • High-pressure injection injury (hydraulic, grease gun): looks small but is a surgical emergency.

Spills

  • Stop the source if safe. Contain with absorbent booms and pads. Cover drains.
  • Report to the control room and environment team.
  • Dispose of used absorbents as hazardous waste.
