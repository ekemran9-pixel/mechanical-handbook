Safety leadership on the floor

Stop work authority, safety conversations, observations, and how a foreman builds a safe crew culture.

  • Stop Work Authority: everyone can stop any job they think is unsafe, without blame. Back them every time, even when they're wrong.
  • Lead by example: wear the PPE, follow the rules, don't take shortcuts when the manager isn't there.
  • Daily toolbox talk: 10 minutes, about today's jobs and hazards, not a generic topic only.
  • Safety conversations: ask open questions at the worksite: "What could hurt you on this job? What would make it safer?"
  • Observations: record safe and unsafe behaviours. Praise good ones publicly, correct unsafe ones privately and immediately.
  • Just culture: honest mistakes are learning opportunities. Reckless behaviour is not tolerated.
  • New workers: buddy them with an experienced worker. Short service workers have higher injury rates.
  • Close the loop: when someone reports a hazard, tell them what was done about it.

Life-saving rules (typical)

  1. Work with a valid permit.
  2. Verify isolation before work.
  3. Obtain authorisation before entering a confined space.
  4. Protect yourself against a fall when working at height.
  5. Never walk under a suspended load.
  6. Obtain authorisation before overriding or disabling safety-critical equipment.
  7. Conduct gas tests when required.
  8. No alcohol or drugs while working or driving.
  9. Follow driving rules (seat belt, no phone, speed).
