Line breaking & opening equipment

Safely breaking into process piping and equipment: preparation, bolt loosening sequence and PPE.

On this page
  1. Preparation
  2. Bolt loosening sequence
  3. Spading and blinding

The first break into a line that held process fluid is one of the most dangerous moments in maintenance. Assume there is trapped pressure and product.

Preparation

  • Isolation per permit (DBB or positive), drained, vented, flushed or purged.
  • Verify depressured: gauge reads zero, open the bleed and leave it open.
  • Know the product and its hazards (SDS). Choose PPE accordingly: face shield, chemical suit, breathing apparatus for toxic fluids.
  • Have a drip tray, absorbent and a hose ready.
  • Gas test the area.
  • Communicate: operations aware and nearby.

Bolt loosening sequence

  1. Position yourself so the flange gap opens away from you.
  2. Loosen the bolts on the far side first (furthest from you), so any release goes away.
  3. Then loosen the nearest bolts last, keeping 2 bolts loose but in place (nuts on a few threads).
  4. Break the joint with a flange spreader (wedge type), never a chisel.
  5. Wait. Watch for leaks. If product comes, retighten and re-isolate.
  6. Remove the remaining bolts only when the joint is clear.

Spading and blinding

  • Spade (blind) rated for the line class, with a handle (tab) painted and tagged.
  • New gaskets on both sides.
  • Record each blind in the blind list (number, location, size, rating, date in, date out).
  • Remove all blinds before return to service, checked against the list.
