Steel erection

Erection sequence, plumbing and levelling, tolerances, temporary bracing, grouting and safety.

On this page
  1. Before erection
  2. Sequence
  3. Tolerances (AISC 303 / EN 1090 guide)
  4. Safety

Before erection

  • Foundations: check anchor bolt positions, projection and threads against the drawing (survey). Anchor bolts: ±3 mm within a group, ±6 mm group to group (AISC typical).
  • Top of concrete levels. Set packers or levelling nuts to base plate underside level.
  • Erection drawings, method statement, lift plans, crane positions and ground checks.
  • Member marks checked on delivery, laid out in erection order on dunnage.

Sequence

  1. Erect columns in a braced bay first. Set on packers, nuts snug.
  2. Fit beams between columns to form a stable box. Fit vertical and plan bracing.
  3. Plumb and align the bay. Tighten bolts.
  4. Extend bay by bay off the stable structure.
  5. Final plumb and level, then final bolt tightening.
  6. Grout base plates (non-shrink grout, poured from one side to drive out air).

Tolerances (AISC 303 / EN 1090 guide)

  • Column plumb: 1:500 of height between splices; for building height max 25 mm toward/50 mm away from building line (AISC, varies by height).
  • Beam level: ±5–10 mm at connections.
  • Distance between columns: ±5 mm.

Safety

  • Never release a member from the crane until it's secured with at least two bolts per connection (OSHA 1926.756) or as the method statement requires.
  • Temporary bracing and guy wires on columns until permanently braced.
  • Fall protection for connectors: harness and lanyard, beam clamps, lifelines, MEWPs.
  • Exclusion zone under the lift.
  • Don't stand on unsecured steel. Check decking fixed before walking.
