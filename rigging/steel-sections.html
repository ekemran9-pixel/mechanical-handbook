Suffix JR, J0, J2, K2 = impact test at +20, 0, −20, −20 °C (K2 = higher energy). Choose for the coldest service temperature.

Weight of plate

Plate mass (kg) = Length (m) × Width (m) × Thickness (mm) × 7.85

A 2 m × 1 m × 20 mm plate = 314 kg.
