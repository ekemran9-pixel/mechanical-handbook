Crane hand signals

Standard crane hand signals (ASME B30 / ISO 16715 style) and radio communication rules.

SignalAction
HoistForearm vertical, forefinger pointing up, small horizontal circles
LowerArm extended down, forefinger pointing down, small horizontal circles
Use main hoistTap fist on head, then use regular signals
Use whip line (auxiliary)Tap elbow with one hand, then regular signals
Raise boomArm extended, fingers closed, thumb pointing up
Lower boomArm extended, fingers closed, thumb pointing down
SwingArm extended, point with finger in direction of swing
StopArm extended, palm down, move arm back and forth horizontally
Emergency stopBoth arms extended, palms down, move arms back and forth horizontally
Dog everything (hold)Clasp hands in front of body
Move slowlyUse one hand to give the signal, other hand motionless in front of it
Extend boom (telescope)Both fists in front, thumbs pointing outward
Retract boomBoth fists in front, thumbs pointing toward each other
Raise boom, lower loadThumb up, flex fingers in and out
Lower boom, raise loadThumb down, flex fingers in and out

Radio rules

  • Dedicated channel for the lift. Test before starting.
  • Continuous talk during blind lifts: "Lower, lower, lower… slow… stop".
  • If the operator loses communication, they stop.
  • Use the crane's call sign before each instruction when more than one crane is working.
