Rigging hardware

Slings, shackles, hooks, eye bolts, beam clamps and spreader beams: types, WLL, inspection and discard criteria.

Slings

TypeProsWatch for
Wire ropeStrong, heat tolerant, cheapKinks, broken wires, birdcage. D/d ratio (bend over small diameters cuts strength)
Chain (Grade 80 / 100)Tough, hot loads, adjustableStretch, nicks, gouges, bent links. Grade 80 marked "8" or "80"
Webbing (flat polyester)Light, protects load surfaceCuts, burns, chemical damage. Colour shows WLL
Round slings (polyester)Flexible, lightCover damage, exposed core yarns

Synthetic sling colour code (EN 1492): violet 1 t, green 2 t, yellow 3 t, grey 4 t, red 5 t, brown 6 t, blue 8 t, orange 10 t and over.

Shackles

  • Bow (anchor) for multi-leg slings; dee (chain) for single in-line loads.
  • Pin types: screw pin (temporary lifts), bolt type with nut and split pin (permanent or long-term, or where the pin can rotate).
  • Load the shackle along its centreline. Side loading reduces capacity (to 70% at 45°, 50% at 90°, per maker).
  • Screw pin fully home, then back a quarter turn is wrong: tighten hand tight, fully seated.
  • Never replace a pin with a bolt.

Eye bolts and hoist rings

  • Shoulder eye bolts only for angled loads, with the shoulder seated flush. Capacity drops sharply with angle (e.g. 25% at 45°).
  • Swivel hoist rings are better for angled lifts.
  • Thread engagement ≥ 1.5 × diameter in steel.
  • Never use plain shank (no shoulder) eye bolts at an angle.

Inspection and discard

  • Pre-use check by the rigger every time. Thorough examination by a competent person (often 6 months for lifting accessories).
  • Discard: missing or illegible tag, cracks, deformation, heat damage, chemical damage, wire rope with 10 random broken wires in one lay or 5 in one strand (ASME B30.9 for slings), chain with 5% wear at link contact or stretch, synthetic slings with cuts or exposed core.
  • Shackles: pin bent, body opening stretched, wear more than 10%.
