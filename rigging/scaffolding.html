Scaffolding & access

Scaffold tags, inspection, safe loads, ladders, MEWPs and the foreman's checks before work at height.

On this page
  1. Scaffold tags
  2. User checks
  3. Ladders
  4. MEWPs (scissor and boom lifts)

Scaffold tags

  • Green: inspected and safe to use. Tag shows date, inspector, load class, and any limits.
  • Yellow (on some sites): modified, special conditions, harness required.
  • Red: incomplete or unsafe. Do not use.
  • Inspected before first use, after any alteration or bad weather, and at least every 7 days (common rule).

User checks

  • Base plates and sole boards on firm ground. No missing base jacks.
  • Guardrails (top rail ~ 1 m, mid rail), toe boards (150 mm).
  • Fully boarded platform, no gaps or trip hazards, boards secured.
  • Safe access (ladder secured, gate or trap door closed).
  • Ties to the structure as designed. Don't remove ties or braces.
  • No materials stacked beyond the load class (light duty 1.5 kN/m², general 2.0, heavy 3.0 kN/m² under EN 12811 classes 2–4).

Ladders

  • Short duration, light work only, with three points of contact.
  • Angle 75° (1 out for every 4 up).
  • Extends 1 m above the landing. Tied or footed.
  • Inspect before use. No painted wooden ladders (hides cracks).

MEWPs (scissor and boom lifts)

  • Trained and authorised operators only.
  • Pre-use check: controls, emergency lowering, tyres, guardrails, alarms.
  • Harness with short lanyard attached to the anchor point in boom lifts (not in scissor lifts unless site rules say so).
  • Check ground and slopes, overhead hazards (crushing against structures is a major killer: anti-entrapment devices).
  • Ground person trained in emergency lowering.
