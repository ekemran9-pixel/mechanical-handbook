Maintenance strategy & KPIs

Reactive, preventive, predictive and RCM strategies, criticality ranking, FMEA, and the KPIs a supervisor should know.

Strategies

StrategyWhat it meansBest for
Run to failure (reactive)Fix when it breaksCheap, non-critical, redundant items (lights, small fans)
Preventive (time-based)Service at fixed intervalsAge-related wear (filters, belts, lubrication)
Predictive (condition-based)Monitor and repair when condition says soRotating machines with random failure patterns
Proactive / precisionRemove causes: alignment, balancing, cleanliness, lubricationEverything
RCMChoose the task for each failure mode based on consequencesCritical systems

Criticality ranking

Score each asset on consequence of failure (safety, environment, production, cost) and probability. Rank A (critical), B, C. Put most PM and condition monitoring effort on A assets. Hold critical spares for them.

FMEA

For each component, list: function → failure mode → effect → cause → current controls. Score Severity, Occurrence and Detection 1–10. RPN = S × O × D. Act on the highest RPNs first.

What a supervisor controls

  • Quality of the work (precision: alignment, torque, cleanliness, fits).
  • Wrench time: kitting, permits ready, tools ready, clear job instructions.
  • Good work order close-out: what was found, what was done, parts used, time. That data drives every KPI.
