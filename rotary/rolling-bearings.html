Rolling element bearings

Reading the number

Basic number: type + dimension series + bore code. For bore codes 04 and up, bore = code × 5 mm. Codes 00, 01, 02, 03 = 10, 12, 15, 17 mm. Bores below 10 mm and above 480 mm are written directly (e.g. 618/500).

6205-2RS1Rubber contact seals both sides (SKF). NSK uses DDU, FAG uses 2RSR

22220 E/C3Spherical roller, 100 mm bore, C3

7312 BECBMAngular contact, 60 mm bore, 40° angle, universally matchable, brass cage

Common suffixes: Z / ZZ / 2Z shields, RS / 2RS seals, N snap ring groove, NR groove with ring, K tapered bore, M machined brass cage, J pressed steel cage, TN9 / P polyamide cage, W33 lubrication groove and holes (spherical), VA / HC / VL0241 special (hybrid ceramic, insulated).

Internal clearance

ClassMeaningUse
C2Less than normalPrecision, low noise
CN (normal)Standard, not markedGeneral
C3Greater than normalElectric motors, interference fits on both rings, warm shafts
C4Greater than C3Hot running: kiln fans, dryer rolls, vibrating screens

An interference fit expands the inner ring and reduces clearance. A hot shaft does the same. Not enough clearance makes the bearing preload itself and overheat.

Fits

Rule: the ring that rotates relative to the load direction needs an interference fit. On most pumps and motors the shaft turns and the load is fixed, so the inner ring is tight on the shaft and the outer ring is a slide fit in the housing.

ApplicationShaftHousing
Electric motors, pumps (ball bearings, normal load)j5 – k5H7 (free end), J7
Medium/heavy roller loadsm5 – m6H7
Heavy load roller bearings, largen6 – p6H7 / J7
Wheel hubs (rotating outer ring)g6 – h6N7 – P7
Angular contact in pump thrust endk5H6 / J6 (no outer ring rotation)

Life

L10 (million revs) = (C / P)^p p = 3 for ball, 10/3 for roller
L10h (hours) = L10 × 1,000,000 ÷ (60 × rpm)

C is the dynamic load rating from the catalogue, P the equivalent load. Halving the load gives 8× the life for a ball bearing. In practice contamination and lubrication decide life more than load. API 610 asks for 25,000 h L10 at rated conditions (and 16,000 h at max loads).

Storage

  • Store flat in original packing, dry, 20 °C, away from vibration.
  • Don't wash the preservative off until fitting (most modern preservatives are compatible with grease and oil).
  • Sealed bearings have a shelf life of grease (often 3–5 years).
  • FIFO stock rotation.
