Fans & blowers

Fan types, laws, inspection, impeller clearance, dampers, balancing needs and blower maintenance.

On this page
  1. Fan types
  2. Fan laws
  3. Inspection points
  4. Axial fans (air coolers, cooling towers)
  5. Roots blowers

Fan types

TypeNotes
Centrifugal, backward curvedEfficient, non-overloading power curve. Clean air, FD fans
Centrifugal, radial bladeDirty, dusty air. Self-cleaning, rugged
Centrifugal, forward curvedLow pressure HVAC. Overloads at high flow
Axial, vane axialHigh flow, low pressure. Cooling towers, tunnels, air coolers
Positive displacement (Roots blower)Fixed volume. Aeration, pneumatic conveying. Needs relief valve

Fan laws

Same as pumps: flow ∝ speed, pressure ∝ speed², power ∝ speed³. Air density changes pressure and power: a cold start on a hot-gas fan (ID fan) draws much more power. Start with the damper closed.

Inspection points

  • Impeller: build-up, erosion of blade leading edges, cracks at welds (blade to back plate). MT or PT on welds.
  • Inlet cone to impeller overlap and radial gap: even all round. Typical gap 0.5–1% of impeller diameter.
  • Shaft seals at the casing.
  • Bearings: plummer blocks, oil level or grease. Locating and floating bearings set correctly.
  • Dampers and inlet vanes: free movement, linkage wear, actuator stroke, full open matches indicator.
  • Flexible connections: tears.
  • Foundation bolts, grout, base.
  • Belt drives: alignment, tension, guards.

Axial fans (air coolers, cooling towers)

  • Blade pitch angle: set all blades the same with a protractor, at the same radius. Typical tolerance ±0.5°.
  • Tip clearance to the fan ring: commonly 0.5% of diameter or the maker's figure, min. 6–10 mm.
  • Hub bolts torqued, blade clamps tight.
  • Vibration switches tested.
  • Gearbox (right-angle) oil level and breather; drive shaft and couplings.

Roots blowers

  • Timing gears keep lobes apart. Check gear backlash and lobe clearances with feelers.
  • Don't run above rated pressure: relief valve on discharge.
  • Oil both gear end and drive end sumps; levels differ.
  • Check silencers and inlet filters.
