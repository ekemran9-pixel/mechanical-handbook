Gearboxes

Gear types, backlash, tooth contact, bearing settings, lubrication and how to read gear tooth damage.

Gear types

TypeNotes
SpurStraight teeth, parallel shafts. Simple, noisy at speed. No axial thrust
HelicalAngled teeth. Quieter, stronger, creates axial thrust. Most industrial reducers
Double helical / herringboneThrust cancels. High power turbine gears
Straight / spiral bevelShafts at 90°. Spiral bevel quieter. Set by shims for contact pattern
Worm and wheelHigh ratio in one stage, low efficiency at high ratios, can be self-locking. Bronze wheel
Planetary (epicyclic)Compact, high torque. Sun, planets, ring and carrier

Backlash

Backlash is the free play between meshing teeth. It allows for oil film, heat and tolerances.

  1. Lock one gear (pinion usually).
  2. Mount a dial indicator on a tooth of the other gear, perpendicular to the tooth, at the pitch circle.
  3. Rock the gear back and forth. Read total movement.
  4. Repeat at 3–4 positions around the gear.

Typical industrial backlash: about 0.03–0.06 × module (mm) for helical gears, more for large or hot gears. Use the maker's figure.

Tooth contact check

  1. Clean teeth with solvent.
  2. Paint 3–4 teeth of the pinion thinly with Prussian blue or marking compound.
  3. Rotate the gears through mesh under light braking.
  4. Read the pattern transferred to the mating gear.
  5. Good: contact over 80% or more of face width and centred about the pitch line.
  6. Contact at one end: misalignment, shafts not parallel, housing distortion, bearing wear.
  7. Contact at tips or roots only: wrong centre distance.

For bevel gears, adjust the pinion position (shims behind the pinion bearing cage) and gear position to centre the pattern.

Bearings and endplay

  • Tapered roller bearings are set with a small endplay (e.g. 0.05–0.10 mm) or preload, using shims under the end cap.
  • Measure endplay with a dial on the shaft end while pushing and pulling.
  • Hot running increases preload in some arrangements. Follow the maker.

Lubrication

  • Use the right ISO VG (often 150–320 for industrial helical, 460–680 for worm) and type (EP mineral, PAO synthetic, PAG for worms only if specified).
  • Fill to the sight glass or dipstick mark with the gearbox stopped.
  • Change interval: 2,500–5,000 h mineral, 10,000 h+ synthetic, or by oil analysis.
  • Clean breathers. Fit desiccant breathers in wet or dusty areas.
  • Oil temperature under about 90 °C mineral, 100 °C synthetic.

Overhaul checks

  • Shaft centre distance and parallelism (housing bore check).
  • All bearings, seals, shims recorded and replaced.
  • Gear runout on the shaft.
  • Housing split face flatness, sealant (anaerobic) on metal-to-metal joints.
  • Clean all magnetic plugs. Look at what's on them before cleaning.
