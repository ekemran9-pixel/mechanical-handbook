Daily reports & records

What goes in a daily report, timesheets, job cards and handover notes, with a template.

Daily report template

  1. Date, shift, area, foreman.
  2. Manpower by trade and hours. Equipment on site.
  3. Work completed (by work order or job number).
  4. Work in progress and % complete.
  5. Problems, delays and their causes (waiting permits, materials, weather).
  6. Safety: incidents, near misses, observations, toolbox talk topic.
  7. Quality: inspections done, NCRs.
  8. Plan for next shift.

Writing tips

  • Facts and numbers: "3 of 8 flanges torqued" not "flanges progressing".
  • Delays with duration and cause: "Waited 2 h for gas test (tester at another job)."
  • Keep it short. One page.
