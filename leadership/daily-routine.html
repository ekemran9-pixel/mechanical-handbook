A foreman's daily routine

A practical hour-by-hour structure for a mechanical shift, from pre-shift prep to handover.

TimeActivity
Before shift (30 min)Read last shift handover, check permits ready, check plan for today, check materials and tools kitted
StartToolbox talk (today's jobs, hazards, lessons learned), allocate crews, confirm permits signed
First hourVisit every job. Check isolations, JSA read, right tools, right people. Unblock anything stuck
Mid-morningMeeting with planner / operations: progress, problems, tomorrow's permits
Before breakSecond walk round. Quality checks (measurements, torque, alignment)
After breakRe-brief crews after the break (fresh look at hazards), check fire watches, gas tests
AfternoonPlan tomorrow: work orders, permits requested, materials, special tools, cranes
Last hourJobs made safe or handed back, permits suspended/closed, housekeeping, tools returned, rods back in ovens
EndDaily report, timesheets, job cards completed, handover to next shift face-to-face

Every walk round, ask

  • Is the permit at the job and is the scope right?
  • Is the area barricaded and tidy?
  • Are people in the line of fire?
  • Are the right tools being used, and are measurements being recorded?
  • What's stopping you from finishing?

The Today page on this site turns this routine into a checklist and builds the shift report for you.
