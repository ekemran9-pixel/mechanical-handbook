Communication & meetings

Talking with operations, engineers, clients and other crafts, running toolbox talks and short meetings.

On this page
  1. Toolbox talks that work
  2. Working with operations
  3. Escalation
  4. Radio etiquette

Toolbox talks that work

  • 5–10 minutes at the start of the shift.
  • Today's jobs and their main hazards. One lesson from a recent event.
  • Ask questions; let the crew talk. Ask the quiet ones.
  • Record attendance.

Working with operations

  • Operations own the plant. Ask before touching anything outside your permit scope.
  • Tell them when you start, when you finish, and if anything changes.
  • Report any leak, abnormal noise or condition you see, even if it's not your job.

Escalation

Escalate early when a job will be late, a safety issue can't be resolved, a quality problem is found, or materials are wrong. Bring the facts and a suggested solution.

Radio etiquette

  • Listen before talking. Short, clear messages.
  • Call sign first: "Control, this is Mechanical 2."
  • Use "over" and "out" (or site standard). Repeat back critical instructions.
  • Emergency traffic takes priority.
