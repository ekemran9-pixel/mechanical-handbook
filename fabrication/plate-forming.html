Rolling, bending & forming

Plate rolling, press brake bending, minimum bend radius, springback and forming limits for pressure parts.

On this page
  1. Plate rolling
  2. Cold forming limits
  3. Press brake bending
  4. Flat pattern (bend allowance)
  5. Hot forming

Plate rolling

  • Three-roll pyramid: leaves flat ends that must be pre-bent (press or rolls) or cut off.
  • Four-roll and double-pinch three-roll: pre-bend the ends in the machine.
  • Roll in several passes, checking with a template (sweep board) cut to the inside radius. Template length at least 1/6 of the circumference or 600 mm.
  • Clean rolls and plate: scale and weld spatter mark the plate.
  • Roll with the plate rolling direction (grain) parallel to the roll axis where possible.

Cold forming limits

For pressure parts (ASME VIII UCS-79), cold forming that strains carbon steel more than 5% may need heat treatment (with conditions). Fibre strain for a cylinder rolled from flat plate:

Strain % = 50 × t ÷ Rf

t = thickness, Rf = final centreline radius. Example: 25 mm plate rolled to 500 mm radius: 50 × 25 ÷ 500 = 2.5%.

Press brake bending

  • Die opening (V) typically 6–8× thickness for mild steel up to ~10 mm; 10–12× for thicker.
  • Inside bend radius about V/6 to V/8 in air bending.
  • Springback: mild steel 1–2°, stainless 2–4°, high strength more. Over-bend to compensate.
  • Minimum inside radius: about 1× thickness for mild steel, 1.5–2× for higher strength or bending across the grain of some plate.
  • Bend with the bend line across the rolling direction (perpendicular to the grain) to reduce cracking.

Flat pattern (bend allowance)

Bend allowance = θ (rad) × (R + K × t)

K-factor about 0.33 for tight bends, 0.5 for large radii (mild steel ~0.4 in air bending). Flat length = sum of straight legs (to the bend tangent lines) + bend allowances.

Hot forming

  • Carbon steel: 900–1,050 °C, work above about 750 °C. Don't work in the blue brittle range (200–400 °C).
  • Heads and fittings formed hot are then normalised.
  • Q&T steels and stainless have special limits.
