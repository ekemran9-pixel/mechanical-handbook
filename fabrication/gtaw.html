GTAW (TIG welding)

Tungsten types, gas, cup sizes, purging, filler rods and technique for TIG roots and stainless pipe.

On this page

  2. Gas
  3. Back purging

Grind DC tungstens lengthwise to a point, about 2–2.5× the diameter long, with a small flat (blunt) tip. Grinding marks must run along the electrode.

Gas

  • Argon is standard. Flow typically 8–12 L/min with a #6–#8 cup; gas lens allows longer stickout.
  • Argon/helium mixes for thick aluminium and copper (hotter arc).
  • Argon + 1–5% hydrogen for some austenitic stainless (faster, cleaner). Not for carbon, ferritic, martensitic or duplex steels.
  • Pre-flow and post-flow: post-flow about 1 s per 10 A protects the tungsten and crater.

Back purging

Stainless, duplex, nickel alloys and titanium need inert gas inside the pipe while welding the root, or the inside oxidises (sugaring).

  1. Make dams (water-soluble paper, inflatable bladders) each side of the joint.
  2. Tape over the joint gap, leaving a small vent.
  3. Purge with argon. Purge volume 5–10× the dammed volume.
  4. Measure oxygen at the vent with an oxygen meter. Common targets: < 0.1% (1,000 ppm) for general stainless, < 50–100 ppm for duplex, < 20–50 ppm for titanium.
  5. Keep purging through the root and hot pass (at least 2 layers or 6 mm of weld).

Heat tint colour on stainless (AWS D18.1/18.2 chart): straw to light gold is usually acceptable; blue, purple, grey and black are not.

Filler rods (AWS A5.18, A5.9)

  • ER70S-2: triple deoxidised. Good for roots on slightly dirty pipe.
  • ER70S-6: high Mn and Si. Common general filler.
  • ER308L for 304/304L, ER316L for 316/316L, ER347 for 321/347.
  • ER309L: carbon steel to stainless.
  • ER2209: duplex 2205.
  • ERNiCr-3 (Inconel 82): nickel alloys and dissimilar joints at high temperature.
  • ER4043, ER5356: aluminium.

Root pass technique (pipe)

  • Gap about 2.5–3.2 mm with 1–1.6 mm land, or per WPS.
  • Walking the cup gives an even bead in the field. Freehand on small bore.
  • Add filler at the leading edge of the pool, keep the rod in the gas shield.
  • Keep a keyhole small and steady for full penetration.
  • Internal reinforcement typically ≤ 1.6–3 mm, no concavity below the pipe surface (check code).
  • Hot pass immediately after root to avoid root cracking.
