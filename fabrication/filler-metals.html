ASME F-numbers and A-numbers

  • F-number groups fillers by usability (welder qualification): F1 (E7024 types), F2 (E6013), F3 (E6010/6011), F4 (E7018 low-H), F5 (stainless SMAW), F6 (all bare wires and rods), etc.
  • A-number groups by deposited chemistry (procedure qualification): A1 carbon steel, A2 C-Mo, A3 Cr-Mo, A8 austenitic stainless.
