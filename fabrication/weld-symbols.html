Weld symbols (AWS A2.4 / ISO 2553)

Reading welding symbols: arrow side and other side, weld types, dimensions, supplementary symbols and the ISO dashed line.

Parts of a welding symbol

  • Reference line: horizontal line, always present.
  • Arrow: points to the joint.
  • Basic weld symbol: the shape (triangle for fillet, V for groove, etc.).
  • Dimensions: size left of the symbol, length and pitch to the right.
  • Supplementary symbols: field weld flag, weld-all-around circle, contour, melt-through.
  • Tail: process, WPS reference, other notes. Left off if nothing to say.

Arrow side and other side (AWS)

  • Symbol below the reference line: weld on the arrow side of the joint.
  • Symbol above the line: weld on the other side.
  • Symbols on both sides: weld both sides.
  • For bevel and J grooves, the arrow has a break pointing to the member to be prepared, if it matters.

ISO 2553 difference

ISO uses a solid reference line plus a dashed identification line. The weld symbol on the solid line = arrow side; on the dashed line = other side. The dashed line can be above or below. ISO fillet sizes use a (throat) or z (leg) before the number, e.g. a5 or z7.

Dimensions

  • Fillet: leg size left of the symbol. Unequal legs: 6×10 (show which is which on the drawing).
  • Groove: depth of prep and effective throat: 10 (12) = prep depth 10, weld size 12.
  • Root opening: inside the symbol. Groove angle: above or below inside the symbol.
  • Length: right of the symbol. Pitch: centre-to-centre spacing after a dash, e.g. 50-150.
  • Chain intermittent: symbols opposite each other on both sides. Staggered: offset symbols.

Supplementary symbols

  • Flag at the junction: field weld (site weld).
  • Circle at the junction: weld all around.
  • Contour: straight line = flush, curved line = convex or concave. Letter after shows finishing method: G grind, M machine, C chip, R roll, H hammer.
  • Melt-through: filled half circle on the opposite side (visible root reinforcement on one-sided welds).
  • CJP / PJP in the tail: complete / partial joint penetration.

Fillet weld size vs throat

Throat (a) = 0.707 × Leg (z) for equal-leg 45° fillets

A 6 mm leg fillet has about 4.2 mm throat. European drawings often give the throat; US drawings give the leg. Check which before welding.
