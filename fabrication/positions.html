What positions qualify (ASME IX, general guide)

  • 1G plate or pipe: flat only.
  • 2G: flat and horizontal.
  • 3G + 4G plate together, or 5G pipe: all positions except horizontal (for pipe), with limits.
  • 2G + 5G, or 6G: all positions.
  • Pipe tests also qualify plate. Plate tests qualify pipe only above a certain diameter (often ≥ 24" OD for ASME; 600 mm for some rules).

Welder qualification (WPQ) basics

  • Welder welds a test coupon to a qualified WPS.
  • Tested by bend tests (face, root, or side bends) or by RT/UT where allowed.
  • Record shows: process, position, F-number, thickness and diameter range qualified, backing, progression (uphill/downhill).
  • Diameter range (ASME IX): test on < 1" OD qualifies the size welded and up; 1" to < 2⅞" qualifies 1" and up; ≥ 2⅞" qualifies 2⅞" and up.
  • Thickness range: a 3/4" (19 mm) or thicker test usually qualifies unlimited deposited thickness.

Continuity

Under ASME IX a welder's qualification lapses if they don't weld with that process for 6 months. Keep a continuity log signed monthly or quarterly. AWS D1.1 also uses a 6-month rule.

Uphill vs downhill

  • Vertical up (uphill): hotter, deeper penetration, thicker beads. Standard for most structural and process piping.
  • Vertical down (downhill): faster, thinner beads. Cross-country pipeline (E6010, E8010) and thin sheet. Changing progression needs a new qualification.
