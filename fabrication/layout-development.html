Layout & pattern development

Marking out, circle and angle geometry, cone and reducer layout, lobster-back bends and pipe branch templates.

On this page
  1. Basic geometry
  2. Cut length of pipe for wraparounds
  3. Concentric cone (reducer) layout
  4. Segmented (lobster-back / mitre) bends
  5. Pipe branch (saddle) template
  6. Marking tools

Basic geometry

Circumference = π × D Chord = 2 × R × sin(θ/2) Arc = R × θ (rad)
  • Divide a circle into 12 or 16 equal parts for pattern work. Chord for 12 divisions = R (radius stepped around).
  • 3-4-5 triangle for squaring: 3 m, 4 m, 5 m (or multiples).
  • Check rectangles by diagonals: equal diagonals = square corners.

Cut length of pipe for wraparounds

Wraparound (template) length = π × OD. Divide into ordinates (e.g. 16 equal strips) for marking profiles.

Concentric cone (reducer) layout

  1. Large diameter D, small diameter d, height H (use mean diameters for thick plate).
  2. Full cone height (to the apex): H' = H × D ÷ (D − d). Slant height of the full cone: L = √(H'² + (D/2)²).
  3. Inner arc radius: l = L × d ÷ D.
  4. Developed angle: θ = 360° × (D/2) ÷ L.
  5. Draw two arcs of radius L and l from the same centre, mark angle θ. That's the flat pattern.

Example: D = 1000, d = 500, H = 400. H' = 400 × 1000 ÷ 500 = 800. L = √(800² + 500²) = 943. l = 471.5. θ = 360 × 500 ÷ 943 = 190.9°.

Segmented (lobster-back / mitre) bends

  • A 90° bend in n segments has 2 end half-segments and (n − 2) full segments, with (n − 1) welds.
  • Cut angle on each segment end = bend angle ÷ (2 × number of welds). For 90° with 3 welds (4 pieces): 90 ÷ 6 = 15° per cut face, each full segment 30° total.
  • Segment back length (outside) = (R + OD/2) × 2 × tan(cut angle); throat (inside) = (R − OD/2) × 2 × tan(cut angle).
  • B31.3 limits mitre bends: angle change per weld over 22.5° is limited to low-pressure service.

Pipe branch (saddle) template

For a branch of radius r on a header of radius R at 90°:

Ordinate height h(θ) = R − √(R² − (r × sin θ)²)
  1. Divide the branch circumference into 16 equal parts (θ = 0°, 22.5°, 45° … 360°).
  2. Calculate h for each θ. The cut line on the branch is h below a straight reference line.
  3. Plot on a paper wrap of length π × OD (branch), join the points smoothly, wrap and mark.
  4. The hole in the header: width = 2r along the header axis, and use the same method on the header.

Example: 4" branch (r = 57.15) on 8" header (R = 109.55): at θ = 90°, h = 109.55 − √(109.55² − 57.15²) = 16.1 mm. At 0° and 180°, h = 0.

Marking tools

  • Soapstone or silver pencil for hot or rough work; scriber and centre punch for accurate lines.
  • Paint markers (low chloride for stainless).
  • Wrap-arounds (flexible steel or fibreglass bands) for square cuts on pipe.
  • Centre head and pipe centring tools for marking branch centres.
  • Laser levels and plumb lines for site layout.
