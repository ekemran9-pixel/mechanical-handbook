GMAW & FCAW (MIG and flux core)

Gas mixes, wire types, settings, stickout, gun angles and common problems with wire processes.

On this page
  1. Shielding gas
  2. Wires
  3. Settings
  4. Common problems

Shielding gas

GasUse
100% CO₂Carbon steel, deep penetration, more spatter. Short circuit and FCAW-G
Ar + 15–25% CO₂ (e.g. 82/18, 75/25)Carbon steel general. Smoother, less spatter
Ar + 5–10% CO₂ (and O₂)Spray and pulse on carbon steel
Ar + 2% CO₂ or Ar + 1–2% O₂Stainless (spray/pulse)
Tri-mix (He/Ar/CO₂)Stainless short circuit
100% Ar, Ar/HeAluminium, copper, nickel alloys

Gas flow 12–20 L/min. Shield from wind above about 8 km/h (2 m/s, AWS D1.1 limit for gas-shielded processes).

Wires

  • ER70S-6 solid wire: most common for carbon steel.
  • E71T-1C / E71T-1M: gas-shielded flux core (C = CO₂, M = mix). All positions. Rutile slag.
  • E70T-5: basic slag, low hydrogen, flat and horizontal.
  • E71T-8: self-shielded, all positions, good toughness. Structural seismic.
  • E71T-11: self-shielded, general light fabrication. Limits on thickness and multi-pass.
  • Metal cored (E70C-6M): high deposition, little slag.

Settings

  • Wire feed speed sets the current. Voltage sets arc length.
  • Stickout: 10–15 mm for short circuit solid wire, 15–25 mm spray, 20–40 mm self-shielded flux core (as the maker says).
  • Longer stickout = lower current, colder weld.
  • Push angle (forehand) 5–15° for solid wire. Drag (backhand) for flux core: "if there's slag, you drag".

Common problems

ProblemCauses
PorosityNo gas (empty bottle, leak, blocked nozzle), wind, too long stickout with gas, contaminated plate
Lack of fusion (cold lap)Short circuit on thick plate, voltage too low, travel too fast, gun angle wrong
Excess spatterVoltage too high, inductance setting, CO₂ gas, dirty contact tip
Erratic wire feedWrong drive roll size or type, liner kinked or dirty, tip worn, spool brake
Burnback to the tipFeed stopped, stickout too short, voltage too high
Bird nest at the rollsToo much roll pressure, liner blocked
Wormhole porosity in FCAWDamp wire, too short stickout on self-shielded

Drive rolls: V groove for solid steel, knurled for flux core, U groove for aluminium. Use a Teflon or nylon liner for aluminium.
