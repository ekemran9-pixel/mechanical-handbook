SMAW (stick welding)

Reading E7018-1 H4R

  • E: electrode. 70: 70 ksi (480 MPa) minimum tensile.
  • 1: all positions (2 = flat and horizontal fillet, 4 = vertical down).
  • 8: coating type and current (low-hydrogen iron powder, AC or DCEP).
  • -1: improved impact toughness. H4: diffusible hydrogen ≤ 4 mL/100 g. R: moisture resistant coating.

Start mid-range, set lower for vertical and overhead (about 10–15% lower).

Technique

  • Arc length: about one electrode core diameter. E7018 needs a short, tight arc; long arc causes porosity.
  • Drag angle: 5–15° in the direction of travel (push uphill for vertical up).
  • Work angle: 45° for fillets, 90° for butt joints.
  • Weave: no wider than 2.5–3× the electrode diameter (codes often limit).
  • Restrikes: on low-H rods, strike ahead in the joint and move back over the crater, or grind the start. Avoid porosity at starts.
  • Arc strikes outside the weld zone are defects. Strike in the joint.
  • Clean slag fully between passes. Grind high spots and steep toes.

Arc blow

Magnetic fields push the arc sideways, mostly with DC near the ends of joints or on magnetised pipe.

  • Switch to AC if the rod allows.
  • Move the earth clamp, or use two earths.
  • Weld toward a heavy tack or toward the earth.
  • Shorten the arc, tilt the electrode against the blow.
  • Demagnetise pipe ends (degaussing coil) or wrap cable around and pass DC to cancel.

Low-hydrogen rod control

  • Store unopened in a dry store. Hermetically sealed cans/vac-packs can be used straight from the pack within the time stated (often 9–12 h).
  • Rebake opened rods: typically 350 °C ± 25 °C for 1–2 h (follow the maker's data sheet). Limit rebakes (often max 2–3 times).
  • Hold in a holding oven at 120–150 °C.
  • Portable quivers on site at ≥ 65 °C, plugged in.
  • Exposure limits in air (AWS D1.1): E70XX 4 h max, higher strength rods shorter. Return to oven or scrap.
  • Don't put cellulosic rods (E6010) in an oven: they need their moisture.
