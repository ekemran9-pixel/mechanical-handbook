Welding processes compared

SMAW, GTAW, GMAW, FCAW and SAW side by side: how each works, strengths, weaknesses and where to use them.

Choosing a process

  • Pipe in the field: GTAW root + SMAW fill and cap. Or GTAW root + FCAW fill.
  • Stainless or alloy pipe: GTAW all through on small bore; GTAW root + SMAW on larger.
  • Structural steel shop: GMAW/FCAW, SAW for beams and long fillets.
  • Structural steel site: SMAW or FCAW-S (wind-tolerant).
  • Thin sheet: GMAW (short circuit or pulse) or GTAW.
  • Pressure vessel seams: SAW with GTAW or SMAW root.

Metal transfer modes (GMAW)

ModeCurrentUse
Short circuit (dip)LowThin material, all positions, roots. Risk of lack of fusion on thick plate
GlobularMediumSpattery, avoid
SprayHigh (needs ≥ 80% Ar)Thick plate, flat and horizontal. Deep fusion
Pulsed sprayPulsedAll positions with spray quality, stainless, aluminium

Polarity

  • DCEP (DC+, reverse): electrode positive. Deeper penetration. Most SMAW rods, all GMAW, most FCAW.
  • DCEN (DC−, straight): electrode negative. GTAW of steels and stainless; some self-shielded FCAW wires; E6013 works well on DCEN.
  • AC: GTAW of aluminium (cleaning action), some SMAW rods (E7018 AC rated), SAW on thick plate to avoid arc blow.
