Temperature

°F = °C × 1.8 + 32 °C = (°F − 32) ÷ 1.8 K = °C + 273.15

Vibration amplitude conversions

  • RMS = 0.707 × peak (sine waves). Peak = 1.414 × RMS. Peak-to-peak = 2 × peak.
  • Velocity (mm/s) = 2π × f × displacement (mm) for peak values.

Viscosity

  • cSt (mm²/s) = cP ÷ density (g/cm³).
  • SSU ≈ cSt × 4.63 (above about 50 cSt).
