NDT methods overview

Which non-destructive test finds which defect, with the limits of each method and personnel qualification.

  2. Personnel

MethodFindsMaterialsLimits
VT visualSurface profile, undercut, cracks, porosity, size, misalignmentAllSurface only. First and most important test
PT liquid penetrantSurface-breaking cracks, porosity, lapsNon-porous: steel, stainless, aluminium, nickelSurface-open only. Temperature 10–52 °C standard
MT magnetic particleSurface and near-surface cracks (to ~2 mm)Ferromagnetic: carbon and low alloy steelNot on austenitic stainless, aluminium. Flaws must cross the field
UT ultrasonic (manual)Lack of fusion, cracks, internal flaws, thicknessMost metalsOperator skill, coarse grains (austenitic welds), thin wall < 6 mm
PAUT phased arrayAs UT, with images and recordsMost metalsSetup and calibration time
TOFDPlanar flaws, accurate height sizingSteels, thicker sectionsDead zones near surfaces
RT radiography (X-ray, Ir-192, Se-75, Co-60)Porosity, slag, incomplete penetration, burn-throughAllRadiation hazard, poor for planar flaws at an angle to the beam
ECT eddy currentTube wall loss, surface cracksConductive, mainly non-ferrousShallow depth
LT leak testingLeak pathsAllPass/fail on leaks only

Which method for which job

  • Butt welds on pipe: RT or UT (PAUT) for volumetric. B31.3 normal fluid service: 5% random RT typical.
  • Fillet and socket welds: VT + MT/PT.
  • Crack check after gouging or before repair: MT (ferritic) or PT.
  • Thickness monitoring: UT thickness gauge.
  • Lamination check on plate before welding nozzles: UT.

Personnel

  • Certified to ISO 9712, ASNT SNT-TC-1A, ACCP or a client scheme.
  • Level 1: performs tests under supervision. Level 2: sets up, performs and evaluates to the procedure. Level 3: writes procedures, trains, approves.
  • Visual acuity: annual near vision test (Jaeger J1 or equivalent) and colour perception.

Timing

  • Delayed NDT for hydrogen cracking: often 24–48 h after welding on thick or high strength steels.
  • After PWHT where the code or spec requires final NDT after heat treatment.
  • Before painting, insulation and pressure testing.
