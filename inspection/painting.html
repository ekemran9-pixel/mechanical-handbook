  • Profile (surface roughness from blasting): typically 50–75 µm for epoxies, 75–100 µm for thick coatings and zinc. Measure with replica tape (Testex) or a profile gauge.
  • Soluble salts: check with Bresle patch test, often max 20–50 mg/m² chloride.
  • Dust: ISO 8502-3 tape test, max rating 2.

Conditions for painting

  • Steel temperature at least 3 °C above the dew point.
  • Relative humidity usually below 85%.
  • Within the paint's min and max application temperatures.
  • Paint blasted steel the same day (before rust bloom, typically within 4 h).

Thickness

  • WFT (wet film thickness) by comb gauge during application.
  • DFT = WFT × volume solids %. Example: WFT 200 µm × 60% solids = 120 µm DFT.
  • Measure DFT with a calibrated magnetic or eddy current gauge. Sampling per ISO 19840 or SSPC-PA2 (e.g. 5 spot readings per 10 m²).
  • Common acceptance: no reading below 80% of specified, average at or above specified, max as the paint maker allows.
