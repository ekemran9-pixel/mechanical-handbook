Glossary

Shop-floor terms in plain words. Use the filter or jump by letter.

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPRSTUVW

A

Accumulation
Pressure rise above the maximum allowable working pressure while a relief valve is discharging.
Accumulator
Vessel that stores hydraulic fluid under gas (nitrogen) pressure, for energy storage or pulsation damping.
Affinity laws
Rules for pumps and fans: flow changes with speed, head with speed squared, power with speed cubed.
Air carbon arc gouging
Removing metal with a carbon electrode arc and a jet of compressed air. Used for back gouging and defect removal.
Alignment
Bringing two coupled shafts onto one common centreline while running.
Anchor (pipe)
Support that stops all movement and rotation of a pipe at that point.
Angular contact bearing
Ball bearing with raceways offset so it carries heavy axial load in one direction. Used in pairs.
Angularity
Angle between two shaft centrelines, measured as gap difference per 100 mm of diameter.
Annealing
Heating and slow cooling to soften metal and remove stresses.
Anti-surge valve
Recycle valve that keeps a centrifugal compressor's flow above its surge line.
Arc blow
Deflection of a DC welding arc by magnetic fields.
Arc strike
Mark left by striking an arc outside the weld joint. A defect that must be removed.
Austenitic stainless
Non-magnetic chromium-nickel stainless steel such as 304 and 316.
Axial float
Total end-to-end movement of a shaft or rotor.

B

Babbitt
Soft tin- or lead-based white metal used to line plain bearings.
Back gouging
Removing the root of a weld from the second side to sound metal before welding that side.
Back pull-out
Pump design where the rotating assembly is removed without disturbing the casing or piping.
Backlash
Free play between meshing gear teeth.
Balance drum
Rotating drum in a multistage pump or compressor that balances axial thrust.
Balancing
Adding or removing mass on a rotor so its mass centre lies on its rotation axis.
Barring
Turning a large rotor slowly by hand or a turning gear, to check freedom or prevent bowing.
Baseline
First set of condition readings on a machine, used to compare later readings.
BEP
Best efficiency point: the flow at which a pump has its highest efficiency.
Bevel
Angled edge prepared on plate or pipe for a groove weld.
Blind (spade)
Solid plate fitted between flanges for positive isolation.
Blowdown
Difference between the set pressure of a relief valve and the pressure at which it reseats. Also the draining of boiler water.
Bolt bound
When a machine cannot move far enough sideways because its bolts touch the sides of the holes.
BPFO / BPFI
Ball pass frequency outer race / inner race: vibration frequencies created by bearing defects.
Brinelling
Dents in bearing raceways at ball spacing, from static overload (true) or vibration while stopped (false).
Bump test
Short test of a gas detector with test gas. Also striking a structure to find its natural frequencies.
Burn-through
Hole melted through the root of a weld.
Butt weld
Weld joining two parts edge to edge in the same plane.

C

Carbon equivalent
Number combining a steel's carbon and alloy content to predict hardening and cracking risk when welded.
Cartridge seal
Mechanical seal pre-assembled on its own sleeve and gland, set at the factory.
Cavitation
Formation and collapse of vapour bubbles where pressure falls below vapour pressure. Damages pump impellers and valves.
Centre of gravity
Point where the whole weight of a load can be considered to act.
Chatter
Rapid opening and closing of a relief valve. Damages the seats.
Clearance (bearing)
Internal free play in a bearing (C2, CN, C3, C4) or the running gap of a plain bearing.
Cold spring
Installing pipe deliberately short or long so thermal growth reduces stress when hot.
Compression packing
Braided rings in a stuffing box, squeezed by a gland to seal a shaft or stem.
Confined space
Enclosed space with limited access, not designed for occupancy, with possible hazardous atmosphere.
Constant level oiler
Bottle that keeps the oil level in a bearing housing at a set height.
Counterbore
Machining the inside of a pipe end to match the bore of the mating part.
Coupling
Device that connects two shafts and transmits torque.
Crater crack
Crack in the crater at the end of a weld bead.
Crush
Interference that clamps a plain bearing shell in its housing.
CUI
Corrosion under insulation.
Cutwater
Tongue of a pump volute where liquid separates to the discharge.

D

DBSE
Distance between shaft ends at a coupling.
Dew point
Temperature at which moisture condenses from air. Steel must be 3 °C above it for painting.
DFT
Dry film thickness of a coating.
Dial indicator
Instrument that measures small movements, used for runout, float and alignment.
Double block and bleed
Two closed valves with an open vent between them, for isolation.
Downcomer
Passage in a distillation column that carries liquid down from one tray to the next.
Dry gas seal
Non-contacting compressor shaft seal where grooves lift the faces on a gas film.
Ductility
Ability of a metal to stretch without breaking.
Dye penetrant (PT)
NDT method where dye seeps into surface cracks and is drawn out by a developer.

E

Eccentric reducer
Reducer with one side flat, used on pump suctions and pipe racks.
Eddy current testing
NDT method using electromagnetic induction, common for exchanger tubes.
Elastomer
Rubber-like material used for O-rings and seals (NBR, FKM, EPDM, FFKM).
Electrofusion
Joining polyethylene pipe with fittings containing heating wires.
End float
Axial movement of a shaft between its thrust limits.
Envelope analysis
Vibration technique that detects repeating impacts, used for early bearing faults.
Erosion
Wear of material by flowing fluid or particles.
Essential variable
Welding variable that, if changed beyond its range, requires a new procedure qualification.
Expansion joint
Flexible element in a pipe or duct that absorbs movement.

F

F-number
ASME grouping of filler metals by usability for welder qualification.
Face runout
Wobble of a face measured by a dial indicator while the shaft turns.
Fatigue
Cracking from repeated stress cycles below the static strength.
Feeler gauge
Set of thin steel blades of known thickness for measuring gaps.
Ferrite number
Measure of the ferrite content of stainless steel weld metal.
Field weld
Weld made at site, not in the shop.
Fillet weld
Triangular weld joining two surfaces at an angle, such as a T-joint.
Fire watch
Person watching for fire during and after hot work.
Flange
Rim on pipe or equipment, bolted to another flange to make a joint.
Flashback arrestor
Device that stops a flame travelling back into gas hoses and cylinders.
Flushing
Circulating fluid through a system to clean it.
FMEA
Failure modes and effects analysis.
Fretting
Wear and corrosion from tiny movements between tight surfaces.

G

Gasket
Seal between two flange faces.
Gear mesh frequency
Number of teeth × shaft speed. A vibration frequency on gearboxes.
Gland
Part that compresses packing or holds a seal in place.
Gouging
Cutting a groove in metal, usually to remove welds or defects.
Grout
Non-shrink material poured under baseplates to give full, even support.

H

HAZ
Heat-affected zone: base metal next to a weld whose properties changed from welding heat.
Head
Energy a pump gives to a liquid, expressed as a height of liquid in metres.
Heat input
Welding energy per unit length of weld, usually in kJ/mm.
Heat tint
Oxide colour on stainless steel from welding heat.
Hi-lo
Internal misalignment between two pipe ends at a weld.
Hold point
Point in a plan where work stops until an inspection is done and released.
Hot work
Any work that can create an ignition source: welding, cutting, grinding.
Hydrogen cracking
Delayed cracking in welds caused by hydrogen, hard microstructure and stress.
Hydrotest
Pressure test with water.

I

Impeller
Rotating part of a pump or compressor that gives energy to the fluid.
Inch-dia
Unit of welding work: pipe size in inches × number of welds.
Incomplete penetration
Weld root not fully fused through the joint thickness.
Inducer
Axial screw ahead of a pump impeller that lowers NPSH required.
Induction heater
Tool that heats bearings or parts by electromagnetic induction.
Interference fit
Fit where the shaft is bigger than the hole, so the parts are tight.
Interpass temperature
Temperature of the weld area between passes.
Isometric
3D piping drawing on 30° axes showing one line with dimensions and materials.
ITP
Inspection and test plan.

J

Jack bolt
Bolt used to push a machine sideways during alignment.
JSA
Job safety analysis.

K

Key
Metal bar fitted in shaft and hub slots to transmit torque.

L

Labyrinth seal
Non-contacting seal made of a series of fine clearances.
Lack of fusion
Weld metal not melted into the base metal or previous pass.
Lantern ring
Spacer in a stuffing box that lets flush liquid reach the packing.
LEL
Lower explosive limit of a flammable gas or vapour.
Lift check
Measuring plain bearing clearance by lifting the shaft with a dial indicator on it.
Line class
Piping specification that sets materials, ratings and components for a line.
LOTO
Lockout tagout: isolating energy and locking the isolation.

M

Magnetic particle (MT)
NDT method using magnetism and iron particles to show surface cracks in ferritic steel.
MAWP
Maximum allowable working pressure of a vessel.
Mechanical seal
Shaft seal with two flat faces, one rotating and one stationary.
Megger
Insulation resistance tester for motors and cables.
Micrometer
Precision tool for measuring dimensions to 0.01 mm or better.
Minimum flow
Lowest flow at which a pump can run continuously without damage.
MTBF
Mean time between failures.
MTTR
Mean time to repair.

N

Nozzle load
Force and moment applied by piping to an equipment nozzle.
NPSH
Net positive suction head: margin of suction pressure above vapour pressure, in metres of liquid.
Nut factor (K)
Factor relating torque to bolt tension, depending mostly on friction and lubrication.

O

Offset
Distance between two shaft centrelines at the coupling.
Oil whirl
Sub-synchronous instability in plain bearings at about 0.42–0.48 × running speed.
Overlap (weld)
Weld metal lying on the base metal without fusing.

P

P-number
ASME grouping of base metals for welding procedure qualification.
Peening
Hammering a weld bead to reduce stresses or distortion.
Permit to work
Written system that controls hazardous work.
Phase (vibration)
Timing relationship of vibration at one point compared to another or to a reference.
Pipe strain
Force from piping pushing or pulling a machine out of alignment.
Plastigage
Crushable plastic thread used to measure bearing clearance.
Plummer block
Split bearing housing that bolts to a base.
PMI
Positive material identification by XRF or spark analysis.
Porosity
Gas pores trapped in weld metal.
Preheat
Heating the base metal before welding to slow cooling.
Preload
Clamping force in a tightened bolt. Also a built-in load on paired bearings.
Priming
Filling a pump and suction line with liquid before start.
PSV
Pressure safety valve.
PWHT
Post weld heat treatment.

R

Radiography (RT)
NDT using X-rays or gamma rays to image the inside of a weld.
RCA
Root cause analysis.
Recirculation
Flow reversal inside a pump impeller at low flow.
Relief valve
Valve that opens in proportion to overpressure, mainly for liquids.
Resonance
Large vibration when a forcing frequency matches a natural frequency.
Reverse dial
Alignment method using two rim dial readings, one from each shaft.
Rider band
Wear band on a reciprocating compressor piston that carries its weight.
Rim and face
Alignment method using one rim and one face dial reading.
Rod drop
Downward movement of a recip compressor piston rod as rider bands wear.
Root face (land)
Flat part at the bottom of a weld bevel.
Root gap
Opening between parts at the root of a weld joint.
Runout
Total movement of a dial indicator on a rotating surface (TIR).

S

Sag (bracket)
Downward bending of an alignment bracket under gravity, corrected in readings.
Seal chamber
Space around the shaft where a mechanical seal fits.
Seal pot
Reservoir for the buffer or barrier fluid of a dual mechanical seal.
Sensitisation
Chromium carbide formation in stainless steel heated to 450–850 °C, reducing corrosion resistance.
Set pressure
Pressure at which a safety valve begins to open.
Shim
Thin plate used under feet to adjust height.
Shrink fit
Assembling by heating the outer part so it shrinks onto the inner part as it cools.
Shut-off head
Head a centrifugal pump makes at zero flow.
Simmer
Audible leakage from a safety valve just below its set pressure.
Slag inclusion
Non-metallic slag trapped in weld metal.
Sling angle
Angle between a sling leg and the horizontal.
Slow roll
Running a rotor at low speed to check runout or warm it up.
Socket weld
Joint where pipe fits into a socket and is welded with a fillet.
Soft foot
Machine foot that does not sit flat on its base.
Spalling
Flaking of material from a bearing or gear surface due to fatigue.
Specific gravity
Density of a liquid compared with water.
Spool
Prefabricated piece of piping.
Spring hanger
Pipe support with a spring that carries weight while allowing vertical movement.
Stonewall
Choke condition of a centrifugal compressor at very high flow.
Strainer
Screen in a pipe that catches debris.
Stress corrosion cracking
Cracking from combined tensile stress and a corrosive environment.
Stuffing box
Chamber around a shaft or stem that holds packing.
Surge
Unstable flow reversal in a centrifugal compressor at low flow.

T

Tack weld
Short weld that holds parts in position before final welding.
Take-out
Centre-to-end length of a fitting, deducted from pipe cut lengths.
Thermal growth
Change in machine dimensions as it heats to running temperature.
Thermography
Measuring and imaging surface temperature with an infrared camera.
Thrust bearing
Bearing that carries axial load.
TIR
Total indicated reading (runout).
Torque
Turning force, measured in N·m or ft·lb.
Tray (column)
Plate inside a distillation column where vapour and liquid contact.
Trim (valve)
Wetted internal parts of a valve: disc, seat, stem.
Turnaround
Planned shutdown of a plant for major maintenance.

U

Ultrasonic testing (UT)
NDT using high-frequency sound to find internal flaws or measure thickness.
Unbalance
Uneven mass distribution on a rotor causing 1X vibration.
Undercut
Groove melted into base metal at the toe of a weld and left unfilled.

V

Vane pass frequency
Number of impeller vanes × running speed.
Vapour pressure
Pressure at which a liquid boils at a given temperature.
Volute
Spiral casing of a centrifugal pump that converts velocity to pressure.

W

Wear ring
Replaceable ring that sets the running clearance between impeller and casing.
Weld neck flange
Flange with a long tapered hub, butt welded to the pipe.
Wire drawing
Erosion of valve seats by high-velocity leakage.
WLL
Working load limit of lifting equipment.
WPS
Welding procedure specification.
Wrench time
Proportion of paid time spent hands-on doing the work.

Field guide figures are typical values for training. The equipment maker's manual, your site standard and the governing code (ASME, API, AWS, ISO, AISC) always take priority.
