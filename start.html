How a topic page works

  1. Title and summaryWhat the topic covers, in one line.
  2. For beginners boxThe idea in simple words, why it matters, an everyday comparison and key words. Read this first if the topic is new to you.
  3. On this pageJump straight to the part you need.
  4. Tables, limits and proceduresThe field facts. Your site standard and the maker's manual always come first.
  5. Diagrams and ▶ PlayPress Play to watch a job step by step; the dots show where you are.
  6. Step by step, mistakes, test yourselfAt the end: the job in order, what people get wrong, and questions. Think of the answer first, then open it.
  7. Previous / NextRead a whole section in order, one page after another.

Install and use offline

Checking this device…

Android

  1. Open the handbook in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Open it once from the new icon while you have internet. Done.

iPhone

  1. Open the handbook in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add, then open it once from the icon.

What you get

  • Opens like an app, full screen, from your home screen.
  • Every page, calculator, quiz and plant guide works with no internet: in the plant, underground or on the road.
  • Uses no mobile data after the first visit; updates come in by themselves when you are online.
  • Your saved calculators, plan progress and shift notes stay on your phone.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up, no adverts.

Do I need internet?

Only the first time. After that it works offline (see above).

Can I trust the numbers?

They are typical field values for training. On the job, your site standard, the approved procedure and the maker's manual always take priority.

Where is my progress saved?

In this browser on this device. Clearing the browser data clears it.

How do I share a page or result?

Copy the page address. In a calculator, the share button makes a link that opens with your numbers filled in.
