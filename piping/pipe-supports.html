Pipe supports

Support types, spans, guides, anchors, shoes, spring hangers and common installation mistakes.

On this page

  2. Typical maximum spans (water-filled, carbon steel, standard wall)

Types

SupportRestrainsUse
Rest / shoeDown only (weight)On racks, insulated lines (shoe lifts pipe above insulation)
GuideLateral movementKeeps the line straight, allows axial movement
Line stop (axial stop)Axial movementDirects thermal growth
AnchorAll directions and rotationFixed points between expansion loops
Hanger (rod)Down only, from aboveOverhead lines
Spring hanger / supportWeight while allowing vertical movementLines with vertical thermal growth near equipment
Snubber / sway braceDynamic loads onlySeismic, water hammer, vibration

Installation tips

  • Install supports exactly as per drawing: type, location and gap. A guide with no gap becomes an anchor.
  • Typical guide gap 1.5–3 mm each side unless specified.
  • PTFE slide plates: keep clean, stainless mating surface on top, no paint on sliding faces.
  • Shoes must be long enough that the pipe can't walk off at full thermal movement.
  • Welded attachments to pipe (trunnions, lugs) are pressure part welds: qualified WPS, and PWHT/NDT if the line needs it.
  • U-bolts: snug only (don't crush the pipe) unless it's an anchor design. Use isolating pads on stainless and coated pipe.
  • Check for missing supports after hydrotest (pipe sagging, pulled equipment nozzles).
