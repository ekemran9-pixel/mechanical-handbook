Spool fabrication & erection

Shop spool fabrication flow, cutting lists, fit-up checks, tolerances, marking, and field erection practice.

On this page
  1. Shop flow
  2. Fabrication tolerances (typical, e.g. PFI ES-3)
  3. Field erection
  4. Spring hangers

Shop flow

  1. Iso received, checked, material issued with heat numbers.
  2. Cut list: pipe lengths, fitting take-outs, bevels.
  3. Cut and bevel. Transfer heat numbers to every cut piece.
  4. Fit-up: check gap, hi-lo, alignment, orientation. QC hold point on critical lines.
  5. Welding per WPS. Weld log updated.
  6. Dimensional check against iso.
  7. NDT per percentage required. PWHT if required.
  8. Internal cleaning, blowout, end caps.
  9. Painting or coating (except weld areas for field test if required).
  10. Spool tagged (spool number, line number, iso) and dispatched.

Fabrication tolerances (typical, e.g. PFI ES-3)

  • Linear dimensions (face to face, centre to face): ±3 mm (±1/8").
  • Flange face tilt: 0.15 mm per 25 mm of flange OD (max 0.4° approx), or 1/16" across face per site spec.
  • Flange bolt hole rotation: ±1.5 mm (1/16").
  • Branch location: ±1.5 mm.
  • Out of plane / overall: ±3–5 mm depending on spool size.

Field erection

  • Erect from fixed points (equipment nozzles) toward the free end. Anchor points first.
  • Set spools on temporary supports. Don't hang spools off equipment nozzles.
  • Use field welds with extra length to cut to fit.
  • Connecting to rotating equipment: make the final flange connection last. Flanges must line up without force (pipe strain check with dial indicators on the coupling). Typical alignment: parallel within 0.25 mm across the face, offset ≤ 0.5 mm, bolts drop in freely.
  • Cold spring only if the design specifies it, with exact gap measured and recorded.
  • Install permanent supports and spring hangers before hydrotest. Spring hangers stay locked (travel stops in) until after hydrotest and insulation.
  • Keep pipe ends capped until connection.

Spring hangers

  • Variable spring: load changes with movement. Cold and hot load marked on the nameplate.
  • Constant spring: same load over full travel.
  • Set to the cold position, remove travel stops after hydrotest and before start-up. Check hot position after start-up and record.
