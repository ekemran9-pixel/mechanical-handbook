Flushing, blowing & cleaning

Water flushing, air blowing, steam blowing, lube oil flushing to ISO cleanliness, and chemical cleaning.

On this page
  1. Methods
  2. Lube oil flushing
  3. Steam blowing
  4. Chemical cleaning steps (typical)

Methods

MethodUseAcceptance
Water flushingWater, process liquid lines. Velocity ≥ 1.5–3 m/sClear water at outlet, no debris in a filter sock or strainer
Air blowingInstrument air, gas lines, small bore. Pressurise to 6–7 bar and dump rapidly (quick-opening valve) repeatedlyWhite painted target or cloth shows no rust or debris
Steam blowingSteam lines to turbinesPolished aluminium or copper target plates: number and size of impact marks within OEM limits (e.g. no marks > 0.8 mm, few above 0.2 mm)
Lube/seal oil flushingTurbomachinery lube systemsISO 4406 particle count (e.g. 16/14/11 or cleaner), patch test, 100-mesh screen clean for set time
Chemical cleaningBoilers, lube systems, stainlessVisual, coupons, final rinse quality
PiggingPipelines, long linesClean pig, gauge plate undamaged

Lube oil flushing

  1. Bypass bearings and seals: install jumpers so flush oil doesn't pass through machine internals.
  2. Use flushing oil (or the service oil) heated to 60–70 °C to lower viscosity.
  3. Aim for turbulent flow: Reynolds number > 4,000 (often flow 2–3× normal using a flushing rig).
  4. Cycle temperature hot/cold, and hammer or vibrate the pipes at welds and bends to loosen debris.
  5. Fit fine filters (5–10 µm) and temporary screens (100 mesh) at bearing inlets.
  6. Sample at set intervals. Continue until particle count reaches the target for the specified time (e.g. 3 consecutive samples at 12 h interval).
  7. Drain flushing oil, clean the reservoir, refill with service oil, remove jumpers, final check flush.

Steam blowing

  • Use temporary piping and a silencer at the outlet, in a fenced exclusion zone.
  • Cycle: heat the line, blow at a disturbance factor (ratio of blow momentum to normal) ≥ 1.2–1.4, then cool. The cooling cycles loosen scale.
  • Install target plates near the end after several blows to check.
  • Noise over 130 dB: notify neighbours, hearing protection.

Chemical cleaning steps (typical)

  1. Degrease (alkaline)
  2. Rinse
  3. Acid pickle with inhibitor (e.g. citric, hydrochloric for carbon steel; nitric/HF for stainless)
  4. Rinse
  5. Neutralise
  6. Passivate
  7. Dry and preserve (nitrogen or VCI)
