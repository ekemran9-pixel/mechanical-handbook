Rules of thumb: 90° LR = 1.5 × NPS (inches), 90° SR = 1.0 × NPS, 45° LR ≈ 0.625 × NPS (4" and up).

Cut length

Pipe cut length = Centre-to-centre − take-out 1 − take-out 2 − weld gaps

Example: two 6" LR elbows 1500 mm centre-to-centre, 3 mm root gaps: 1500 − 229 − 229 − 6 = 1036 mm.

Elbows at other angles

Take-out = R × tan(θ / 2) R = 1.5 × NPS × 25.4 for LR

6" LR elbow cut to 60°: 228.6 × tan 30° = 132 mm.

Reducers

  • Concentric: centrelines match. Vertical lines and general use.
  • Eccentric: one side flat. Pump suctions (flat side on top to avoid air pockets), pipe racks (flat on bottom to keep bottom of pipe level).

Socket weld and threaded (ASME B16.11)

  • Classes 3000, 6000, 9000 (socket weld) and 2000, 3000, 6000 (threaded).
  • Usually NPS 2 and smaller.
  • Socket weld gap: pull the pipe back about 1.6 mm (1/16") from the socket bottom before welding to allow for shrinkage. Use a gap ring or mark the pipe.
  • Fillet weld size: B31.3 asks for a minimum leg of 1.25 × nominal pipe wall, or the socket wall thickness if that is smaller.
  • Not for severe cyclic service or where crevice corrosion matters.

Branch connections

  • Tees (equal and reducing): best for strength.
  • Weldolet (butt weld), sockolet (socket), threadolet (threaded), elbolet (on elbows), latrolet (45°), nipolet.
  • Stub-in (set-on or set-in branch) with or without reinforcing pad, per code calculation.
  • Branch hole: cut and bevel to give full penetration access. Remove the coupon.
