Material verification

  • Check mill test certificates (EN 10204 3.1 or 3.2) against the heat number on the item.
  • Colour coding by site standard (e.g. painted stripe per material).
  • PMI (XRF) on alloy materials before installation.
  • Quarantine and tag any material without traceable marking.
