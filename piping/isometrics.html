Reading P&IDs & isometrics

How to read P&IDs and piping isometrics: symbols, line numbers, north arrow, dimensions, BOM and weld numbering.

On this page
  1. P&ID (piping and instrumentation diagram)
  2. Isometric drawing
  3. Common isometric symbols
  4. Checking an iso before fabrication

P&ID (piping and instrumentation diagram)

  • Shows the process: equipment, lines, valves, instruments and control loops. Not to scale.
  • Line number format often: size – service – sequence – line class – insulation, e.g. 6"-P-1023-A1A-H.
  • Line class (piping spec): tells you materials, rating, gaskets, bolts, valve types and branch table for that line.
  • Instrument bubbles: letters show the function (FT flow transmitter, PI pressure indicator, TIC temperature indicating controller, LSHH level switch high-high, PSV pressure safety valve).
  • Valve symbols: gate (bow tie), globe (bow tie with a dot), ball (bow tie with a circle), check (with arrow or Z), butterfly, control valve (with actuator on top).
  • Spec breaks shown with a symbol where the line class changes.

Isometric drawing

  • 3D view of a single line (or part) drawn on 30° axes. Not to scale, but fully dimensioned.
  • North arrow shows plant north. Lines run along N–S, E–W or up–down.
  • Coordinates (N 1200, E 2350) and elevations (EL +105.300 or TOS, BOP, COP) locate the line in the plant.
  • BOP = bottom of pipe, COP/CL = centreline, TOS = top of steel.
  • Dimensions: centre-to-centre or face-to-face as shown, in mm or feet-inches.
  • Bill of materials (BOM): every component with size, rating, material spec and quantity.
  • Weld numbers: shop welds and field welds (FW). Field weld often marked with extra length (e.g. +100 mm) for site fit-up.
  • Spool numbers: the pieces fabricated in the shop and shipped to site.
  • Supports: tag numbers and types on the iso, details on separate support drawings.
  • Continuation: "Continued on drawing …" at each end.

Common isometric symbols

SymbolMeaning
Dot on lineShop weld
Dot with flag or "FW"Field weld
Two short parallel lines across pipeFlanged joint
Triangle on pipeReducer (concentric/eccentric with flat side shown)
Hatching or slope arrowLine slopes (e.g. 1:100 toward drain)
"PS" or support tagPipe support
Vent/drain symbols (HPV, LPD)High point vent, low point drain

Checking an iso before fabrication

  1. Latest revision? Check the revision cloud and title block.
  2. Line class and BOM match the spec.
  3. Dimensions add up: sum of segments = overall dimension between coordinates.
  4. Valve orientation (stem, flow direction) and handwheel access.
  5. Branch and instrument connection sizes and ratings.
  6. Weld numbering, NDT requirements and PWHT notes.
  7. Field weld locations sensible for transport and fit-up.
