Gaskets

Spiral wound, sheet, ring joint, kammprofile and other gaskets: selection, colour codes and handling.

On this page
  1. Spiral wound (ASME B16.20)
  2. Colour codes (B16.20)
  3. Ring joint gaskets (API 6A / ASME B16.20)
  4. Other types
  5. Handling

Spiral wound (ASME B16.20)

  • V-shaped metal strip wound with soft filler (graphite or PTFE).
  • Outer (centring) ring: carbon steel usually. Centres the gasket on the bolts, acts as a compression stop.
  • Inner ring: required for PTFE filled gaskets, for NPS 24 and up in Class 900, NPS 12 and up in Class 1500, NPS 4 and up in Class 2500, and recommended generally. It stops buckling of the windings into the bore.
  • Thickness 4.5 mm (winding), compressed to about 3.2–3.4 mm.

A stripe on the outer ring shows the filler: grey = flexible graphite, white = PTFE, light green = ceramic, pink = mica-graphite. The gasket is also stamped with the size, class, winding metal and filler, so read the stamping as well as the colours.

Ring joint gaskets (API 6A / ASME B16.20)

  • R: oval or octagonal. Octagonal seals better and is common. Oval fits old round-bottom grooves too.
  • RX: pressure-energised, for API 6A flanges.
  • BX: pressure-energised for 10,000 psi+ API 6BX flanges, face-to-face contact.
  • Ring must be softer than the groove (soft iron, low carbon steel, 4–6% Cr, 304, 316). Hardness stamped on the ring.
  • Never reuse ring joints.

Other types

  • Kammprofile (camprofile): serrated metal core with graphite or PTFE facing. Excellent for heat exchangers and high-cycle services.
  • Compressed non-asbestos fibre (CNAF): water, low pressure steam, utilities. Class 150 to 300 low temperature.
  • Flexible graphite sheet (with metal insert): high temperature, good chemical resistance, not for strong oxidisers.
  • PTFE (filled or expanded): chemicals. Cold-flows, needs good bolting.
  • Rubber: water, FF flanges.
  • Insulating gasket kits: cathodic protection isolation. Sleeves and washers for each bolt.
  • Double jacketed: heat exchanger girth flanges with pass partitions.

Handling

  • New gasket every time.
  • Check the size, class and material stamping against the line class or spec.
  • Store flat, in the original packaging. Don't hang spiral wound gaskets on nails.
  • No grease or compound on spiral wound or graphite gaskets unless the maker says so.
  • Gasket must be centred: the outer ring touches the bolts.
