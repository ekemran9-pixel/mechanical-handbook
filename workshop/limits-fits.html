Limits, fits & tolerances

ISO hole-basis fits, common fit classes for bearings, couplings and bushings, and reading GD&T basics.

On this page
  1. Fit types
  2. Common fits (hole basis)
  3. Example values (nominal 50 mm)
  4. IT grades
  5. Basic GD&T symbols
  6. Surface finish

Fit types

  • Clearance fit: always a gap (H7/g6, H7/h6). Sliding parts.
  • Transition fit: might be a small gap or small interference (H7/k6, H7/js6). Location, keyed hubs.
  • Interference fit: always tight (H7/p6, H7/s6). Pressed or shrunk parts.

Capital letter = hole, small letter = shaft. Number = tolerance grade (IT). Lower number = tighter. H holes have zero lower deviation.

IT grades

  • IT5–IT7: precision machining (bearing seats, grinding).
  • IT8–IT9: good turning and milling.
  • IT11–IT13: rough machining, cutting.

Surface finish

Ra in µm. Typical: rough turning 6.3–12.5, finish turning 1.6–3.2, grinding 0.4–0.8, lapping 0.05–0.2. Bearing seats often Ra 0.8–1.6. Seal sleeve running surface 0.2–0.4.
