Fitting keys

  • Side fit: snug, light tap fit in the shaft (N9 or P9 keyway), sliding or snug in the hub (JS9).
  • Top clearance: always a gap between the key top and the hub keyway bottom (0.1–0.4 mm). A tight top pushes the hub off-centre and causes runout.
  • Key length: no longer than the hub; ends rounded or chamfered.
  • Use key steel (bright drawn, often C45), not soft bar.
  • Damaged keyways: never weld up a keyway on a critical shaft without engineering approval.

Other keys

  • Woodruff: half-moon, tapered shafts, small drives.
  • Gib head taper key: driven in, the gib head allows extraction. Guard it, it's a snag hazard.
  • Splines: multiple teeth, high torque, sliding hubs (gearboxes, PTOs).

Keyless shaft connections

  • Taper-lock (QD) bushes: tighten grub screws evenly in steps to torque. Use the jacking holes for removal.
  • Shrink discs: outside the hub, squeeze it onto the shaft. Tighten bolts in a circular sequence in several passes until all reach torque. Faces of the discs must stay parallel.
  • Locking assemblies (Ringfeder, Fenner Trantorque): expanding rings between shaft and hub. Clean, oil (not moly unless specified), tighten in passes.

Retaining rings and set screws

  • Circlips: internal and external. Use the right pliers. Sharp edge faces the load. Don't over-expand.
  • Set screws: cup point on a flat or into a dimple. Thread locker. Two screws at 90° for better hold.
