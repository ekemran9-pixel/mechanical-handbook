Cooling towers

Cooling tower parts, fan and gearbox care, fill and drift eliminators, water treatment and Legionella control.

  • Induced draft counterflow: fan on top, air up, water down through fill. Most common.
  • Crossflow: air horizontal through fill, water down.
  • Fill: film fill (PVC sheets) or splash fill. Fouling reduces performance.
  • Drift eliminators: stop droplets leaving with the air.
  • Distribution: spray nozzles or hot water basins.

Mechanical equipment

  • Right-angle gearbox: oil level (often via external sight glass and fill line), oil changes, vibration switch.
  • Drive shaft: composite or steel, with flexible couplings. Check alignment and coupling elements.
  • Fan blades: pitch, tip clearance, hub bolt torque.
  • Motor outside the airstream on most towers.

Water and health

  • Chemical treatment against scale, corrosion and biological growth.
  • Legionella risk: towers need a written risk assessment and control plan, monitoring, and regular cleaning and disinfection.
  • Wear a respirator (FFP3/N95 minimum) when working in a tower with fans running or water circulating.
