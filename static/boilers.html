Boilers (basics)

Fire-tube and water-tube boilers, mountings, water treatment basics and safety checks for the mechanical team.

On this page
  1. Types
  2. Mountings
  3. Water treatment basics
  4. Tube failures
  5. Repair

Types

  • Fire-tube: hot gas through tubes inside a water shell. Up to about 25 bar and 30 t/h steam. Packaged boilers.
  • Water-tube: water and steam inside tubes, gas outside. High pressure and capacity. Drums, risers, downcomers, superheaters.
  • HRSG: heat recovery steam generator on gas turbine exhaust.

Mountings

  • Safety valves (at least two on most boilers), set at or below MAWP.
  • Gauge glasses (two independent level indications), blown down each shift.
  • Low water cut-offs: tested regularly by draining the float chamber.
  • Main steam stop valve, feed check valve, blowdown valves (surface and bottom).
  • Pressure gauge, temperature gauges.

Water treatment basics

  • Hardness causes scale. Dissolved oxygen causes pitting. Deaerator plus oxygen scavenger.
  • Blowdown controls total dissolved solids (TDS).
  • pH typically 10.5–11.5 in boiler water for low pressure boilers.

Tube failures

  • Short-term overheating (bulging, fish-mouth rupture with thin edges): low water, blocked tube.
  • Long-term overheating (thick-lipped rupture, creep): scale inside, flame impingement.
  • Corrosion fatigue, caustic gouging, hydrogen damage, oxygen pitting.
  • Erosion from soot blowers or fly ash.

Repair

Boiler tube repairs follow ASME Section I / NBIC, with qualified welders, approved WPS, NDT and hydrotest. Tube pad welds and window repairs need engineering approval.
