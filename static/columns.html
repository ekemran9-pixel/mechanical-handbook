Columns & trays

Distillation column internals, tray types, packing, manway entry, tray inspection and installation checks.

On this page
  1. Internals
  2. Tray inspection
  3. Packing installation
  4. Entry

Internals

  • Trays: sieve, valve (fixed or moving), bubble cap. Each tray has an active area, downcomer, weir and inlet area.
  • Packing: random (rings, saddles) or structured (corrugated sheets). Needs liquid distributors and bed supports.
  • Demister pads, chimney trays, collector trays, feed distributors.

Tray inspection

  • Count and check all tray manways are closed on reassembly (from bottom up, often with an inspector).
  • Valve units: free moving, not stuck or missing.
  • Weir height and levelness: within about ±3 mm across the tray (check with a water level or laser).
  • Downcomer clearance (gap under downcomer to the tray below): per drawing, typically ±3 mm.
  • Bolts and clamps tight. Gaskets in place where specified.
  • Corrosion, fouling, plugged holes.

Packing installation

  • Structured packing: install layers rotated 90° to each other. Don't walk on it without boards.
  • Random packing: fill with water first, or lower in bags, to avoid breaking it. Level each layer.
  • Distributors must be level: uneven liquid distribution kills performance.

Entry

  • Full blinding of all connections. Purge and ventilate (blowers at the top or bottom manways).
  • Pyrophoric iron sulphide deposits can ignite as they dry in air. Keep wet, remove promptly.
  • Rescue plan for heights inside the column.
