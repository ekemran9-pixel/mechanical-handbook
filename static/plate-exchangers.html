Plate heat exchangers

Gasketed plate exchangers: opening, plate cleaning, gasket replacement and the tightening dimension.

On this page
  1. Opening
  2. Cleaning and gaskets
  3. Closing
  • Plates with gaskets clamped between a fixed frame plate and a pressure plate by tie bolts.
  • Very efficient, compact. Narrow channels foul easily: use strainers upstream.

Opening

  1. Isolate and drain both sides. Let it cool.
  2. Measure and record the tightening dimension (A): distance between frame plate and pressure plate. Also on the nameplate.
  3. Mark the plate pack with a diagonal line with paint, so the order can be checked.
  4. Loosen bolts gradually and evenly, diagonally, keeping the pressure plate parallel.
  5. Slide plates back along the carrying bar one at a time.

Cleaning and gaskets

  • Clean plates with soft brushes and water. Never use steel brushes or wire wool on stainless or titanium. Use approved chemicals only (no chlorides on stainless).
  • Check plates for cracks and holes with dye penetrant or light box.
  • Clip-on gaskets: fit into the groove, all clips engaged. Glued gaskets: remove old glue, use the maker's adhesive.
  • Gaskets must sit fully in the groove.

Closing

  • Hang plates in the right order and orientation (the diagonal paint line shows mistakes).
  • Tighten bolts evenly, diagonally, keeping plates parallel, to the A dimension. Never go below the minimum A: it crushes the plates.
  • Leak test before returning to service.
