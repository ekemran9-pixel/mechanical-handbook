Valve types

Gate, globe, ball, plug, butterfly, check, diaphragm and needle valves: design, use, and how they fail.

Bonnets

  • Bolted bonnet: most common.
  • Pressure seal bonnet: high pressure steam. Internal pressure tightens the seal.
  • Welded bonnet: small forged valves.
  • Extended bonnet: cryogenic service.
  • Bellows seal: zero emissions, toxic services.

Trim

Trim = the wetted moving and seating parts (stem, disc/wedge, seat rings, back seat). API 600 trim numbers specify materials: Trim 8 (13Cr + hardfaced Stellite seats) is common for general hydrocarbon service; Trim 5 = full Stellite; Trim 12 = 316 SS.

Body markings (MSS SP-25)

Body shows: maker, pressure class, material (e.g. WCB, CF8M, LCC), size, flow arrow (if directional). Tag plate shows trim, API standard, serial number.

Common failures

  • Gate valves: seat leakage from wire-drawing (throttled), stem packing leaks, thermal binding (hot closed then cooled).
  • Ball valves: soft seat damage from solids, stem seal leaks, cavity overpressure (trapped liquid heating up) on non-relieving designs.
  • Butterfly: liner damage, disc edge erosion.
  • Check valves: hinge wear, disc slam, sticking.
