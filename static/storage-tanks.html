Storage tanks (API 650/653)

Tank types, roofs, seals, inspection, floor and shell repair rules, settlement and hydrotest.

On this page
  1. Types
  2. Floating roof items
  3. Inspection (API 653)
  4. Repairs
  5. Vacuum box test

Types

  • Fixed cone or dome roof: low vapour pressure products, water. Fitted with breather valves (PVRV) and flame arrestors.
  • External floating roof: crude, gasoline. Pontoon or double deck. Primary and secondary rim seals.
  • Internal floating roof: a floating deck under a fixed roof. Reduces emissions.

Floating roof items

  • Rim seals: mechanical shoe, foam-filled or liquid-mounted. Check gaps (measured with a gauge rod).
  • Roof drain (articulated pipe or hose): test for leaks, keep the valve open in rain.
  • Roof legs: set to operating or maintenance position. Landed roof means vapour space under it.
  • Pontoons: check for liquid by opening inspection hatches.
  • Rolling ladder and gauge pole.

Inspection (API 653)

  • Routine in-service visual by operators (monthly).
  • External inspection by an authorised inspector: at least every 5 years.
  • Internal inspection: based on floor corrosion rate, normally max 20 years (longer with RBI).
  • Floor: MFL (magnetic flux leakage) scan, UT spot checks, vacuum box test on welds.
  • Shell: UT thickness, check minimum thickness per course.
  • Settlement survey: edge and planar tilt.

Repairs

  • Floor patch plates: lap welded, min. size and spacing per API 653.
  • Shell door sheet (insert plate): full penetration welds, RT.
  • Hydrotest after major repairs (shell repairs, new bottom) unless exempt under API 653.

Vacuum box test

  1. Clean the weld, apply soap solution.
  2. Place the box with a soft gasket over the weld.
  3. Pull vacuum of at least 21 kPa (3 psi) gauge, hold at least 5 seconds.
  4. Watch through the window for bubbles. Overlap each position by at least 50 mm.
