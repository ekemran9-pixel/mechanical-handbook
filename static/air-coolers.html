Air coolers (fin fans)

Air-cooled heat exchanger parts, header plugs, fan maintenance, fin cleaning and tube leak repair.

On this page
  1. Parts (API 661)
  2. Header plugs
  3. Fin cleaning
  4. Fan checks

Parts (API 661)

  • Tube bundle with finned tubes (aluminium fins, L-foot, embedded G-fin, or extruded).
  • Headers: plug type (a threaded plug opposite each tube, for access), cover plate type (bolted cover), manifold / bonnet.
  • Fans (forced draft below the bundle, or induced draft above), fan ring, plenum.
  • Drives: belt or right-angle gearbox, electric motor. Some with variable pitch hubs or VFDs.
  • Louvres for temperature control.

Header plugs

  • Plugs give access to each tube for cleaning, inspection and plugging.
  • Use new plug gaskets every time (soft iron, stainless or as specified).
  • Torque to specification. Over-torquing cracks or galls the threads.
  • Number and map the plugs on the header.

Fin cleaning

  • Low-pressure water or air from the clean side (opposite to air flow), a few bar only. High pressure flattens fins.
  • Foam cleaners for oily deposits.
  • Comb straightened fins where bent.
  • Dirty fins can cut performance 20–30% in summer.

Fan checks

  • Blade pitch equal on all blades.
  • Tip clearance to the fan ring (API 661: no more than 0.5% of fan dia, max 19 mm; at least 6 mm).
  • Belt alignment and tension, or gearbox oil.
  • Vibration switch function test.
  • Fan guard and hub bolts secure.
